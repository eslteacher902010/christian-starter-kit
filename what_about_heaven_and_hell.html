Lesson Eight

What About Heaven and Hell?

The Bible Describes Judgment Day

(Matthew 13:47-50 NKJV) "Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a dragnet... the angels will come forth, separate the wicked from among the just, and cast them into the furnace of fire. There will be wailing and gnashing of teeth."

(Revelation 20:11-15 NKJV) "Then I saw a great white throne... and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works... And anyone not found written in the Book of Life was cast into the lake of fire."

The Fixed-Up & Made New World

(Revelation 21:1-4 NKJV) "Now I saw a new heaven and a new earth... And God will wipe away every tear from their eyes; there shall be no more death, nor sorrow, nor crying. There shall be no more pain, for the former things have passed away."

Two Destinations

God is holy and must deal with sin. Sin can be dealt with by us through repentance, faith, and following Jesus in the pursuit of holiness. This way of dealing with sin leads to heaven.

Or sin can accumulate and "store up the wrath of God for the Day of Judgment". People who have not dealt with their sin by repentance, faith, and following Jesus will be destroyed—cast into the lake of fire. Hell is not a sort of debauched party full of sinners having a good time; it is unrelenting anguish without any place for or possibility of rest and relief.

The Joy of Heaven

Heaven is the absolute enjoyment of the presence and power of God. It is where God's love is perfectly understood and where absolute total blissful happiness is normal. The days of suffering, meaninglessness, misfortune, and calamity are over.

  • • Every scar on your soul is healed.
  • • Every tear is forgotten.
  • • You will reign with Christ in humility.
  • • The life of God flows through you like an ever-flowing stream.
