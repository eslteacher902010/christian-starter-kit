Lesson Six

Meeting God's People

The First Church

(Acts 4:32-35 NKJV) "Now the multitude of those who believed were of one heart and one soul; neither did anyone say that any of the things he possessed was his own, but they had all things in common... and they distributed to each as anyone had need."

A New People - A People of God

Being a Christian means being part of a "new people" (Ephesians 2:15, 2 Cor 5:17) we are introduced to the "household of God" (Ephesians 2:19) and have brothers and sisters in the faith. The Holy Spirit dwells in the midst of God's people making it a "temple of the Holy Spirit".

God uses different Christians in different ways. Some are powerful preachers, others help the poor, while others are good at praying or administration. We are like the parts of a body (1 Corinthians 12:1-11). The Bible calls the church "the body of Christ"—we are the visible, on-this-earth representation of Jesus Christ.

A Good Church

  • Sound biblical preaching lifting up Jesus.
  • Humble, godly, and wise leadership.
  • The people clearly love one another.
  • Holiness and clear standards of right and wrong.

A Bad Church (Watch Out)

  • Claims to be the "only group" saved.
  • Refuses to be up-front on first meeting.
  • Centered around one "infallible" human leader.
  • Does not see Jesus as the only way to salvation.
  • Occult practices or immorality.
  • Has "their own version" of the Bible.

Loving the Church

Once you have found a good church, stick with it despite the ups and downs. Churches are full of people like you who make mistakes. Don't get bitter because of a foolish one or two. Here are three things you should take to church each Sunday:

  • 1. Your heart: Go with an open and expectant attitude to be filled with the Spirit.
  • 2. Your eyes and hands: Watch for the needs of God's people and meet them in practical ways.
  • 3. Your mind: Hear, understand, and think about God's word. Faith and an enquiring mind go hand in hand.
