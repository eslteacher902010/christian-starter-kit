Lesson Three

How To Pray

"Lord, teach us to pray..." (Luke 11:1)

The Lord's Prayer

(Matthew 6:5-18 NKJV) "And when you pray, you shall not be like the hypocrites... But you, when you pray, go into your room, and when you have shut your door, pray to your Father who is in the secret place; and your Father who sees in secret will reward you openly."

"In this manner, therefore, pray:"

Our Father in heaven, Hallowed be Your name.
Your kingdom come. Your will be done On earth as it is in heaven.
Give us this day our daily bread.
And forgive us our debts, As we forgive our debtors.
And do not lead us into temptation, But deliver us from the evil one.
For Yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever. Amen.

Basic Truths About Prayer

  • 1. Believe that God hears what you pray, even in secret.
  • 2. Do not pray for public show, but to be heard by God.
  • 3. Maintain a "balanced prayer diet." Pray for God's honor, His Kingdom, our basic needs, forgiveness, and deliverance.
  • 4. Use the Lord's Prayer as a "backbone." Let each phrase trigger specific requests for your life, your church, or your friends.

Where, When and How To Pray

When Jesus prayed, it was often:

Alone in a quiet place.
With deep emotion and intimacy.
With persistence and force.
With godly fear and reverence.

Find a quiet place, take your Bible, and pour out your heart to God. The words are not that important; God understands you. Just speak in a way that is respectful and truthful, yet bold—He is our Father and He wants us to speak what is in our hearts to Him.
