Lesson Nine

What About My Friends?

"Evil company corrupts good habits." (1 Corinthians 15:33)

When Friends Lead You Astray

(James 4:4-5) "Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God? Whoever therefore wants to be a friend of the world makes himself an enemy of God."

(2 Cor 6:14) "Do not be unequally yoked together with unbelievers. For what fellowship has righteousness with lawlessness? And what communion has light with darkness?"

These verses are amongst the hardest in the Bible and they are there for a purpose. It is very easy to end up in relationships that are counterproductive to our Christian faith. Let's review the key points:

1

Corrupting Habits: Bad company soon draws new Christians away from their relationship with Christ and back into the world. Recognize bad company—and avoid it.

2

The Pull of "The World": By "the world," the Bible means the place where people clamber for status and pride without reference to God. Living for material gain or sensual pleasure is incompatible with the Spirit of God.

3

The Unequal Yoke: This applies to marriage, close friendships, and business partnerships where you must "pull in the same direction." Close ties with unbelievers can compromise your walk because they do not have the spiritual strength to resist the Evil One.

A Father's Promise

Satan wants to "steal, kill and destroy" (John 10:10). By keeping distance from damaging relationships, you are not judging people; you are obeying God. He promises a deeper relationship with Him if you follow His lead:

"Therefore 'Come out from among them and be separate,' says the Lord... 'I will be a Father to you, and you shall be My sons and daughters,' says the LORD Almighty." (2 Cor 6:17-18)

Applying this requires courage and wisdom. Putting distance between yourself and a person does not mean hating them. You are to love them with a cautious and wise love that refuses to get involved beyond the boundaries set by God.
